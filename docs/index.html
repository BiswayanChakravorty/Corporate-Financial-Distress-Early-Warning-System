Methodology controls

The controls that matter most in financial distress research.

POINT-IN-TIME AVAILABILITY No feature may use information unavailable at prediction time. FORWARD LABELING The target represents entry into a predefined distress state after t. TEMPORAL VALIDATION Training precedes the out-of-time test period. MODEL DISCIPLINE Synthetic observations are never presented as empirical evidence. NEXT RESEARCH STEP Freeze a documented public-company dataset and independently sourced distress events before publishing real-world performance.
